Privacy Policy

This page explains how “Little Stationmaster” (“the app”) handles information.

Information we collect

The developer does not collect personal information such as your name, email address or location, and the app sends nothing to the developer’s servers.

However, the app uses Google AdMob to show ads, and AdMob may collect advertising identifiers, device information, information about ads shown and tapped, your IP address (used to estimate your general location), and diagnostic information about how the app runs. For details, see How Google uses information.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress and settings are stored on your device and are never sent to the developer’s servers. You can erase them from the app’s settings, and they are erased when you delete the app.

If your device’s backup is turned on (such as Google backup and device-to-device transfer on Android, or iCloud Backup on iOS), the saved data may be included in that backup. Backups are made by your device and its operating system; the developer never receives them.

To move to a new device, you can make a “Transfer code” in the app’s settings. The code is your game progress and some settings written out as text. It is made on your device and sent nowhere. Copying and pasting it uses the clipboard, which the app reads only when you tap “Paste”. Anyone who has your code can load your progress, so don’t share it. Purchases are not in the code; use “Restore purchases” on the new device to get them back.

Ads and purchases

Ads in the app are rewarded ads that you choose to watch. You can play the whole game without watching them. Depending on where you live, the app shows an ad consent screen, and on iOS a tracking permission screen. If you don’t allow it, you’ll see ads that are not based on your interests. Where consent is required, you can change your answer from “Ad consent” in the app’s settings.

In-app purchases such as “Remove ads” and the station staff (like the “Driver”) are handled by the App Store or Google Play. The developer never receives your payment information.

Notifications

Only if you allow notifications, the app schedules these reminders on your device: when offline earnings reach their limit, when the freight train has finished loading, on the morning the station hotel’s guests check out, and when today’s missions have arrived. At most two a day, and none between 9 p.m. and 8 a.m. Reminders are cancelled when you come back to the app. No information is sent anywhere for notifications. You can turn them off in the settings at any time.

Review requests

After you have played for a while, the app may show the rating screen provided by the App Store or Google Play (a few times a year at most). Ratings and reviews go to the store, not to the developer’s servers.

Children

The developer does not collect information from children either. For ads, see the AdMob section above.
